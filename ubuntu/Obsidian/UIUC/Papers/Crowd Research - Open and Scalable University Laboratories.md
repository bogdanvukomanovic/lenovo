**Question 2.** How did this group decide on the order of authors? 
> The paper states different strategies for determining author order (alphabetization, single join author, etc.) and provides reasons why the provided strategies are not fair/would fail. Crowd Research technique introduces the decentralized credit system, which considers every participant's opinion in determining credit. The following steps summarize the mentioned system:
> - Each participant is given 100 credit points that they can allocate to the other participants based on their assessment of who impacted the project (submitting strong milestones, collaborating actively, being Directly Responsible Individual (DRI), etc.)
> - These credit allocations create weighted directed graph
> - Modified PageRank algorithm is applied to the created graph
> - Acquired PageRank scores are used to rank participants
> 
> Principal investigator and DRIs work together to set a threshold score for co-authorship. Participants with scores:
> - above the threshold are listed as coauthors
> - under the threshold are credited in acknowledgments
